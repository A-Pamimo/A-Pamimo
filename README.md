## 👋 About

I'm an economics researcher working at the intersection of **innovation, development, and applied econometrics**. My work spans causal-inference research (difference-in-differences, event studies) and the data tooling that makes empirical work faster and more transparent.

- 🔬 **Research interests:** innovation & knowledge diffusion, development economics, high-frequency macro measurement
- 📈 **Methods:** DID / event-study designs, panel data, reproducible research pipelines in **R**, **Python**, and **Stata/TeX**
- 🛠️ **Also:** I build products — when a workflow is slow or a dataset is hard to see, I ship a tool for it
- 🎯 **Currently:** preparing for graduate study in economics
